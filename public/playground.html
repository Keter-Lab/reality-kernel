How to read the response

      

        

          
Divergence

          
Low divergence usually indicates the requested action stays inside a stable basin. Higher divergence means downstream effects are pulling toward destructive or ambiguous state changes.

        

        

          
Evidence

          
Evidence labels summarize why the engine escalated or blocked a request: destructive writes, intent divergence, egress mismatch, privilege shifts, session escalation patterns.

        

        

          
Operator workflow

          
ALLOW continues automatically. WARN moves into operator review with an explicit approve/block decision. BLOCK terminates execution and preserves the proof hash for downstream audit.
